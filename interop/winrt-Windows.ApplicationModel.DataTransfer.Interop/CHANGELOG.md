# Changelog

What changed in `winrt-Windows.ApplicationModel.DataTransfer.Interop`, which

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- The `winrt.windows.applicationmodel.datatransfer.interop` module, for sharing
  from a desktop app: `get_for_window()` gets the `DataTransferManager` of a
  window, and `show_share_ui_for_window()` shows the share sheet for it.
